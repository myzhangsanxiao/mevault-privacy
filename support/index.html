MeVault Support

Last updated: November 6, 2026

MeVault keeps your photos, videos, files, passwords and notes encrypted on your iPhone. It has no account and no servers, so everything below happens on your device.

Contact

Email jiangsuqq@hotmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version (Settings → Version). Never send us your passwords, backup files or backup keys. We will never ask for them.

I forgot my password. Can you reset it?

No. Your password is never stored and we have no copy of your keys, so no one, including us, can reset it or recover your data. The only option is to delete the app, install it again and create a new vault. If you exported a backup, you can import it again with the main password you set when exporting it.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

The vault is not included in iCloud or computer backups, so it does not move automatically. Before switching:

  • Go to Settings → Export Backup to save your passwords and notes as an encrypted backup file, then import it on the new iPhone with Settings → Import Backup.
  • Backups do not include photos or files. Export them first, then import them again on the new iPhone.
  • Importing a backup requires the full version. If you already bought it, open Settings → Full Version on the new iPhone and tap Restore Purchases. You won’t be charged again.

Why does Face ID ask for my password?

For your safety, you need to enter your password once after the Face ID validity period runs out (Settings → Unlock Protection), after your iPhone restarts, or after the date and time are changed. Face ID works again right after.

What is "Auto-Destroy Data"?

It is off by default. If you turn it on, the vault is erased after the number of wrong passwords in a row that you choose. The unlock screen warns you when 5 or fewer attempts are left, and failed Face ID attempts do not count. Erased data cannot be recovered, so export a backup first.

My backup won't open

  • Enter the main password you set when you exported that backup. It may be different from your unlock password.
  • If the backup has an extra key, you also need the backup key (or the second passphrase) you saved at that time. The backup key is not case-sensitive and the dashes are added for you.
  • Without these, the backup cannot be opened by anyone.

What does the full version include?

Everything in MeVault is free except importing backups, which requires the full version. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Exporting backups is always free.

What happens if I delete the app?

All of its data is deleted from your iPhone and cannot be recovered. Export a backup before deleting the app.

Privacy

MeVault does not collect any data and does not connect to the internet. Read the full Privacy Policy.

保管员 支持

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 6 日

保管员（英文名 MeVault）把你的照片、视频、文件、密码和笔记加密保存在 iPhone 上。本应用没有账号，也没有服务器，下面所有操作都只在你的设备上进行。

联系我们

请发邮件到 jiangsuqq@hotmail.com，并写明 iPhone 型号、iOS 版本和 App 版本（设置 → 版本）。请不要把密码、备份文件或备份密钥发给我们，我们也绝不会向你索要。

忘记密码了，能帮我重置吗？

不能。密码不会被保存，我们也没有你的密钥，任何人（包括我们）都无法重置密码或找回数据。唯一的办法是删除 App 后重新安装，新建一个保险箱。如果你导出过备份，可以用导出时设定的主密码重新导入。

换新 iPhone 怎么办？

保险箱不包含在 iCloud 和电脑备份里，不会自动转移。换机前请：

  • 在 设置 → 导出备份 把密码和笔记导出成加密的备份文件，在新 iPhone 上用 设置 → 导入备份 导入。
  • 备份不包含图片和文件。请先把它们导出，再在新 iPhone 上重新导入。
  • 导入备份需要完整版。如果你已经买过，在新 iPhone 上打开 设置 → 完整版，点“恢复购买”即可，不会重复收费。

为什么面容 ID 要我输入密码？

为了安全，以下情况需要先输入一次密码：超过了面容 ID 有效期（设置 → 解锁保护）、iPhone 重启后、修改过日期和时间后。输入一次后面容 ID 就恢复使用。

“数据自动销毁”是什么？

默认是关闭的。打开后，连续输错密码达到你设定的次数，保险箱会被清空。剩下 5 次以内时解锁页会提醒，面容 ID 失败不计入次数。清空的数据无法恢复，打开前请先导出备份。

备份打不开

  • 请输入导出这份备份时设定的主密码，它可能和你的解锁密码不同。
  • 如果这份备份设置了额外密钥，还需要当时保存的备份密钥（或二级口令）。备份密钥不分大小写，横线会自动加上。
  • 缺少这些，任何人都无法打开这份备份。

完整版包含什么？

除了导入备份，保管员的所有功能都免费。导入备份需要完整版，一次购买，不是订阅。导出备份始终免费。

删除 App 会怎样？

App 里的全部数据会从 iPhone 上删除，无法恢复。删除前请先导出备份。

隐私

本应用不收集任何数据，也不联网。完整内容请看 隐私政策。
