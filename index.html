MeVault Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

MeVault (the "App") is an encrypted vault that stores your photos, files, emails and notes on your own device. This policy explains what the App does and does not do with your information.

1. We do not collect your data

The App does not collect, transmit, sell, share or analyze any personal data. Everything you save in the App stays on your device, encrypted. We have no servers that receive your data, and we cannot see, access or recover it.

2. No network connection

The App does not use the internet to function and contains no networking features. It includes no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting services, and no third-party SDKs that collect information.

3. Permissions the App may request

  • Camera: used only to take photos that go straight into your vault. They are not saved to your Photos library.
  • Photos (add only): used only when you choose to export an item from your vault to your Photos library.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: used to unlock your vault. Biometric data is handled entirely by iOS; the App never receives or stores your face or fingerprint data.

4. Encryption and your password

Your content is protected with strong encryption using keys derived from your password and, where available, your device's hardware security. Because we never have access to your password or keys, we cannot reset your password or recover your data if you forget it. Please keep your password safe and make backups.

5. Backups and exports

If you create a backup or export a file, it is written to the location you choose. Where you store or send those files is under your control, and the App does not upload them anywhere.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: jiangsuqq@hotmail.com

保管员 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 5 日

保管员（英文名 MeVault，以下简称"本应用"）是一款加密保险箱，在你自己的设备上保存照片、文件、邮件和笔记。本政策说明本应用如何对待你的信息。

1. 我们不收集你的数据

本应用不会收集、传输、出售、共享或分析任何个人数据。你保存在应用中的所有内容都以加密形式留在你的设备上。我们没有接收你数据的服务器，也无法查看、访问或找回你的数据。

2. 不联网

本应用运行不需要网络，也不包含联网功能。应用中没有广告、统计分析、崩溃上报，也没有任何会收集信息的第三方 SDK。

3. 可能请求的权限

  • 相机：仅用于拍摄直接存入保险箱的照片，这些照片不会保存到系统相册。
  • 照片（仅添加）：仅在你主动把保险箱中的内容导出到系统相册时使用。
  • 面容 ID / 触控 ID：用于解锁保险箱。生物特征数据完全由 iOS 系统处理，本应用不会获取或保存你的面容或指纹数据。

4. 加密与你的密码

你的内容使用强加密保护，密钥由你的密码派生，并在条件允许时结合设备的硬件安全能力。由于我们无法获取你的密码或密钥，如果你忘记密码，我们无法重置密码，也无法找回你的数据。请妥善保管密码并做好备份。

5. 备份与导出

你创建备份或导出文件时，文件会写入你选择的位置。这些文件存放或发送到哪里由你自己决定，本应用不会把它们上传到任何地方。

6. 儿童

本应用不面向儿童，也不会有意收集任何人的信息。

7. 政策变更

如本政策有更新，我们会在本页面发布新版本并更新日期。

8. 联系我们

对本政策有疑问，请联系：jiangsuqq@hotmail.com
