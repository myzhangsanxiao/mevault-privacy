English简体中文 MeVault Privacy Policy Last updated: November 6, 2026 MeVault (the "App") is an encrypted vault that stores your photos, videos, files, passwords (email, bank card and app accounts) and notes on your own device. This policy explains what the App does and does not do with your information. 1. We do not collect your data The App does not collect, transmit, sell, share or analyze any personal data. We have no servers that receive your data, and we cannot see, access or recover it. 2. Your data stays on this device Everything you save in the App is encrypted and stored only on this device. The vault is excluded from iCloud and computer backups of your device, so it is not uploaded with them. If you delete the App or move to a new phone, your data can only be restored from a backup file you exported yourself (see section 8). 3. No network connection The App does not use the internet to function and contains no networking features. It includes no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting services, and no third-party SDKs that collect information. If you tap a link, such as a website saved in a password entry or the link to this policy, it opens in Safari. If you buy the full version, the purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store; we never receive your payment details, and the app only asks the system whether you have purchased it. 4. Permissions the App may request Camera: used only to take photos that go straight into your vault, and to scan the QR code of a backup recovery key. Nothing is saved to your Photos library. Photos (add only): used only when you choose "Save to Photos", for example to export items from your vault or to save a backup recovery key card as an image. The App cannot read your Photos library. Importing photos: when you import from your Photos library, you pick the items in the system photo picker; the App receives only the items you choose and does not need access to your library. Face ID / Touch ID: used to unlock your vault. Biometric data is handled entirely by iOS; the App never receives or stores your face or fingerprint data. 5. Encryption and your password Your content is protected with strong encryption using keys derived from your password and, where available, your device's hardware security. Your password itself is never stored. Because we never have access to your password or keys, we cannot reset your password or recover your data if you forget it. Please keep your password safe and make backups. 6. Clipboard When you copy an account, password or card number, it stays on this device only (it is not shared with your other devices through Universal Clipboard) and is cleared automatically after 30 seconds. 7. Opening and sharing files To open some documents, or to share or export files, the App briefly creates an unencrypted temporary copy on this device. It is deleted when you finish, when the App goes to the background or is locked, and each time the App starts. 8. Backups and exports Backup files you export are encrypted with a password you set, and optionally with an extra key. If you create a backup or export a file, it is written to the location you choose. Where you store or send those files is under your control, and the App does not upload them anywhere. Please do not send backup files through chat apps or email, because their servers may keep copies. 9. Deleting your data You can permanently delete everything in Settings with "Erase all data". If you turn on "Auto-Destroy Data", the vault is erased after the number of wrong password attempts you choose. Deleting the App also deletes all of its data from this device. 10. Children The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. 11. Changes to this policy If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. 12.
